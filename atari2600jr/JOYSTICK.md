🏆 The Best Options for a Good Atari Joystick Today
1. A refurbished original Atari CX40

This is the gold standard for authenticity.

A properly rebuilt CX40 feels:

    tight but smooth

    with a crisp microswitch‑like click from the dome contacts

    and a solid, weighty shell

You can rebuild them with:

    new gold‑plated contacts

    new rubber boot

    new centering ring

    new fire button plunger

A restored CX40 is way better than any modern clone.
2. The Hyperkin “Trooper” (surprisingly decent)

If you want something new but not junk, the Hyperkin Trooper is one of the few modern sticks that isn’t terrible.

It’s:

    heavier

    more solid

    has a better button feel

    and doesn’t fall apart

It’s not identical to a CX40, but it’s respectable.
3. The CX40+ (Atari’s modern reissue)

Atari released a modern reproduction for the 2600+.

Pros:

    feels close to the original

    good build quality

    brand‑new parts

Cons:

    not exactly the same internals

    availability varies

Still, it’s one of the best “new” options.
4. Go premium: Edladdin Super 78 or Seagull 78

If you want a joystick that feels like a real arcade stick:

    metal enclosure

    Sanwa or Seimitsu arcade parts

    ultra‑precise

    built like a tank

These are expensive but absolutely top‑tier.
