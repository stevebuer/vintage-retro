## Parts

🎯 RG‑6 Compression Connectors (Broadcast‑Grade)
F‑Type Male (RG‑6)

These are the same connectors used by cable installers — extremely reliable.
PPC / Belden EX6XL

    Digi‑Key: PPC‑EX6XL‑ND

    Mouser: Not typically stocked — Digi‑Key is the better source

This is the gold standard. If you want the best, get this one.
Belden Snap‑N‑Seal SNS1P6

    Digi‑Key: 1528‑1013‑ND

    Mouser: Not commonly stocked

Also excellent — fully compatible with quad‑shield RG‑6.
🎮 RCA Male Compression Connector (RG‑6)

These are harder to find in single quantities, but here are the good ones:
Belden / PPC RCA6 (RCA Compression for RG‑6)

    Digi‑Key: 1528‑1020‑ND

    Mouser: Usually not stocked

This is the correct, high‑quality RCA connector specifically for RG‑6.
It’s solid metal, 75‑ohm, and perfect for Atari RF.
Ideal Industries 89‑071 (RCA RG‑6 Compression)

    Mouser: IDEAL 89‑071

    Digi‑Key: Not typically stocked

Ideal’s compression connectors are very reliable and easy to work with.
🔌 RCA → F Adapter (High‑Quality, 75‑Ohm)

You want a solid metal, machined adapter — not the hollow stamped ones.
Switchcraft 3502F (RCA Plug to F‑Jack)

    Digi‑Key: SC1098‑ND

    Mouser: 502‑3502F

Switchcraft = top‑tier machining and plating. This is the one to get.
🧰 Strain‑Relief Boot for RCA Connector

These make the RCA end look clean and protect the cable when you’re plugging/unplugging often.
Switchcraft 48 Boot (Fits RCA Plugs)

    Digi‑Key: SC1128‑ND

    Mouser: 502‑48

This is the classic rubber boot used on pro‑audio RCA cables.
📎 Optional: Ferrite Choke (Clip‑On)

Helps kill diagonal interference from modern electronics.
TDK ZCAT2035‑0930A

    Digi‑Key: 445‑2402‑ND

    Mouser: 810‑ZCAT2035‑0930A

Fits RG‑6 perfectly.
